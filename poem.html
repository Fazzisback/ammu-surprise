To My Ammu, Kisho ❤️

Across the miles that stretch and pull,
Through empty space and quiet nights,
My world becomes entirely full
The moment your eyes catch the light.


They hold a warmth that guides me home,
A brilliant, deeply striking grace,
The sweetest gaze I've ever known,
That blurs the miles of time and space.


I love you more than words can trace,
More than the distance we must brave,
And in the mirror of your face,
Is every future that I crave.


So look at me across the screen,
And know my heart is safely there;
The most beautiful eyes I've ever seen,
Answering my every prayer.

Forever Yours ❤️
Your Baby, Kavi
